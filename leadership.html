Event Management · Final Year

Workshop Organizer

JNNCE Shivamogga · 2023–2024

Overview

During my final year at JNNCE, I took the initiative to organize a technical workshop for fellow students. This was a hands-on event designed to bridge the gap between classroom theory and industry-relevant skills. From conceptualization to execution, I was responsible for every aspect of the event — making it one of the most formative experiences of my academic life.

Key Responsibilities

  • 📋
    Planning & Scheduling

    Designed the workshop agenda, set timelines, and coordinated with faculty advisors for approvals and venue bookings.

  • 🎤
    Speaker Coordination

    Reached out to and briefed technical speakers and resource persons, ensuring content aligned with participant skill levels.

  • 📣
    Promotion & Outreach

    Handled all event promotion within the campus — posters, announcements, and registration management.

  • 🛠️
    Logistics Management

    Managed venue setup, equipment requirements, seating, and day-of operations to ensure a smooth experience for all participants.

  • 🤝
    Team Coordination

    Led a small team of volunteers, delegating tasks and ensuring everyone stayed on track with the event schedule.

Outcomes & Impact

✅

Successfully organized the workshop from scratch with zero prior event management experience.

👥

Coordinated participation from multiple departments, bringing diverse perspectives to the event.

🏆

Received positive feedback from participants and faculty, with the event being recognized as a notable campus initiative.

📈

Built skills in planning, stakeholder management, team leadership, and real-time problem-solving.

Skills Demonstrated

Event Planning Team Leadership Communication Logistics Stakeholder Management Problem Solving Time Management

State Level · Academic Event

Vice Chair

State-Level Academic Event · JNNCE Shivamogga

Overview

Being selected as Vice Chair for a state-level event was a significant responsibility that tested my organizational, communication, and leadership skills at scale. This role placed me at the center of coordinating across multiple colleges, teams, and stakeholders — far beyond the scope of a typical college activity.

Key Responsibilities

  • 🎖️
    Executive Decision Making

    Participated in high-level planning meetings, contributing to agenda-setting, schedule decisions, and issue resolution.

  • 📡
    Inter-college Coordination

    Served as a primary point of contact between participating institutions, handling inquiries and ensuring smooth information flow.

  • ⚖️
    On-Ground Oversight

    Managed day-of activities, resolved last-minute issues, and ensured the event ran on schedule and within planned parameters.

  • 📝
    Reporting & Documentation

    Maintained documentation of event proceedings, participant data, and prepared summaries for faculty and department heads.

  • 🤲
    Guest & Dignitary Management

    Assisted in managing the hospitality and scheduling of guest speakers, judges, and faculty members from across the state.

Outcomes & Impact

🌟

Gained hands-on experience operating in a structured, high-accountability environment at state level.

🤝

Built a professional network spanning multiple colleges and departments across the state.

💬

Sharpened public communication and interpersonal skills by working with diverse groups of students and faculty.

🔍

Developed a keen eye for detail and operational awareness needed to manage complex multi-team events.

Skills Demonstrated

Executive Leadership Cross-team Coordination Public Communication Accountability Documentation Conflict Resolution Professionalism

✦ Reflection

What Leadership Taught Me

🧭

Direction Under Uncertainty

Real events rarely go exactly as planned. I learned to stay calm, adapt quickly, and make sound decisions under pressure — a skill I carry into every technical challenge.

🗣️

Communication is Everything

Whether coordinating a team or briefing a speaker, clear and confident communication was the most powerful tool I had. I became more intentional about how I share information.

🔗

Collaboration over Competition

The best outcomes came from lifting others, not competing with them. Building trust within a team makes execution exponentially more effective.

📐

Attention to Detail

Large-scale events succeed or fail on the small details. I developed the habit of thinking systemically while staying operationally sharp on the specifics.
