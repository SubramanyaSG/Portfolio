📚 Academic & Foundation

Starting B.E. in Information Science & Exploring System Architecture

Entering JNNCE Shivamogga marked the beginning of an exciting chapter. I immersed myself in the fundamentals of computer science and information engineering, focusing heavily on Data Structures, Object-Oriented Programming, and computer systems.

Early on, I developed an intense curiosity about how networked systems communicate and how vulnerabilities arise in code. Rather than just passing exams, I spent evenings experimenting with Linux environments and exploring how low-level systems function under the hood.

💡 Core Realization: Reliable cybersecurity starts with a rock-solid understanding of programming architecture, memory management, and network protocols.

🔒 Cybersecurity Mastery

Earning 3 Major Cybersecurity Certifications & Security Labs

2023 was the turning point where my academic direction solidified into Cybersecurity. I committed to rigorous independent training alongside my college syllabus, earning 3 verified certifications in Ethical Hacking, Cryptography & Network Security, and Practical Cyber Security.

I practiced packet inspection, network defense mechanisms, encryption algorithms (AES, RSA), and vulnerability scanning. This period gave me practical confidence beyond textbooks and set me apart from peers.

🤖 AI Research & Event Management

Text-to-Speech with Emotion Control + Lead Workshop Organizer

In 2024, I spearheaded two major milestones: our major technical project in Deep Learning Speech Processing, and taking on the primary organizer role for a department-wide technical workshop.

Our project focused on generating emotionally modulated speech synthesis using neural models. Simultaneously, organizing the campus workshop tested my ability to manage guest speakers, venue logistics, registrations, and lead a student volunteer team under strict timelines.

🎯 Dual Milestone: Proved that I could innovate in deep neural audio generation while commanding logistics and team coordination in real-world campus events.
