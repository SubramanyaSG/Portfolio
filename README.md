# Portfolio

## Run locally

1. Open a terminal in this folder.
2. Run `D:/miniconda/python.exe -m http.server 3000`.
3. Open `http://localhost:3000` in your browser.

## What is included

- Hero section with your name and cybersecurity focus
- About section with your degree background
- Certifications section for 4 cybersecurity certifications
- Featured project section for your text-to-speech work
- Leadership section for the workshop and vice chair role
- Contact section for your links and resume
